What I Built & Led

  • Co-founded and lead Trac (previously MacTransit), a real-time mobile platform serving 2,500+ weekly active users.
  • Designed and launched Commuter, Driver, and Admin experiences across Android and iOS while personally writing more than 75% of the backend.
  • Grew the project from a McMaster capstone into a shuttle-tracking business entering its second year.
  • Secured a B2B partnership with Attridge, expanding deployment and validating the product’s commercial value.
  • Architected a Firebase real-time event pipeline processing vehicle telemetry every 5 seconds and coordinating session state, ETA/occupancy updates, stale-vehicle detection, authentication, and synchronization across commuter and driver clients.
  • Developed and deployed a multiple linear regression arrival model using Python and scikit-learn, achieving ~30-second average prediction error from historical stop-level wait times and operational data.
  • Used separate algorithms for vehicle processing: Kalman filtering to smooth noisy real-time GPS and A* to calculate stop-level routing distance.
  • Lead product vision, customer onboarding, business operations, financials, outreach, and end-to-end engineering.
