What I Built

  • Developed a Gipps car-following simulation across 4 Raspberry Pi vehicles, maintaining approximately 5–10 cm following gaps.
  • Designed and tuned a PID controller with real-time OpenCV camera processing, improving path-following accuracy from roughly 60% to 90–95%.
  • Implemented an MQTT communication protocol for reliable interaction between multiple vehicles.
  • Simulated a traffic manager that coordinates intersection flow and helps vehicles navigate shared routes.
